## Candidates considered

**Freelance Videographer Booking & Project Tracking** - Template marketplace flooded with videography/filmmaker solutions. Evidence: Multiple "Video Creator" templates on Notion marketplace, dozens of videography contract templates on Jotform. Rejected due to high template saturation and difficulty differentiating from existing photographer/videographer solutions.

**Salon/Barber/Hairdresser Scheduling & Inventory** - Highly saturated market with extensive template coverage. Evidence: 10+ booking website templates on ThemeForest, multiple Notion booking OS templates, widespread inventory spreadsheets on Smartsheet/Jotform. Rejected due to extreme template saturation and low differentiation opportunity.

**Handyman/Contractor Job Tracking** - Rejected due to YMYL adjacency risk—construction/repair work involves building codes, safety regulations, and permitting that could invite professional advice claims. Content angle cannot stay purely operational without ethical friction.

**Freelance Social Media Manager & Content Calendar** - Content calendar templates widely available. Evidence: "9 Free Must-Have Content Calendar Templates," "15 Free Content Calendar Templates" on ClickUp, Asana, Hive, Teamwork, Zoho, Canva. Rejected due to oversaturation of freely available templates and high competition from content management platforms.

**Wedding Planner/Coordinator** - Rejected because wedding planning is effectively covered by general event planning template collections, which were identified as saturated. Notion Marketplace shows "Top 10 Dream Wedding Planner Templates" collections with extensive existing coverage.

**Freelance Graphic Designer Project Tracking** - Moderate template competition. Evidence: Generic freelance templates exist (Flowlu, Asana, Lark design project templates), and design-specific project management templates available on multiple platforms. Secondary candidate but templates like "Freelance Management" and "Design Project Management" are widely available. Rejected in favor of stronger market opportunity.

**Podcast Producer & Episode Management** - **SELECTED.** Global podcasting market valued at USD 32-62 billion in 2026 with projected CAGR of 25-28.9% through 2031. Creator economy podcast monetization reached $44 billion in 2026. Evidence: Market research shows explosive growth fueled by video podcasts, smart speaker integration, streaming platform adoption, and diversified monetization. Web search revealed moderated template availability—existing templates on Gumroad (Podcast Producer $4.99, Podcast Production HUB) exist but market nowhere near saturation of photographer/salon/social media/event planner niches. Clear, specific operational pain points: guest coordination and scheduling (tracking multiple guest outreach, confirmations, prep materials), episode production timeline management (recording → editing → publishing → promotion on separate schedules), sponsorship deal tracking and affiliate link management, equipment and recording resource inventory, and multi-show management for podcasters running parallel projects.

## Why this niche

The podcasting industry is experiencing explosive growth (25-28.9% CAGR through 2031) as part of the creator economy boom, with a multi-billion dollar addressable market significantly larger than previous niche candidates. The market size, growth trajectory, and creator momentum are unambiguous: podcasting reached $44B in creator monetization (2026) with migration of linear TV budgets, smart speaker integration, and video podcast expansion driving sustained demand. Podcast creators face specific, painful operational needs that a template solves well: coordinating multiple simultaneous guest outreach pipelines (offers, confirmations, prep material delivery), managing complex production workflows with separate recording/editing/publishing/promotion timelines, tracking sponsorship deal terms and affiliate commissions across episodes, organizing recording equipment and guest intake across multiple shows. The niche has moderate, not saturated template competition—a couple of existing solutions exist (Podcast Producer at $4.99, Podcast Production HUB) but nowhere near the saturation of photographer/salon/event planner/social media calendar markets. Content angle stays entirely in operational territory: episode planning workflows, guest coordination systems, production timeline organization, sponsorship tracking mechanics, release schedule management, multi-show organization—never touching podcast production technique, audio engineering, or editorial decisions. The revenue model matches niche A: a well-designed Notion template ($18-25) + a supporting blog with operational guides ("how to coordinate multiple podcast guests," "episode production workflow from recording to release," "multi-show organization system," "sponsorship deal tracking") drives traffic to a Gumroad listing.
