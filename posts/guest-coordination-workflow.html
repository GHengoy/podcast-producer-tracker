Day 4

  • Five stages. Every guest moves through outreach, confirmation, preparation, recording, and completion.
  • Capture data at each stage. Note confirmation status, prep sent, and recording date and time.
  • Email doesn't scale. One shared place prevents double-bookings and forgotten follow-ups.

The Podcast Producer & Episode Management Tracker has guest name, email, and confirmation status columns next to recording and publish dates, so every guest's stage is visible. Every guest gets one row, so nothing hides inside an old email thread.
